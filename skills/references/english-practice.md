# English practice sentences: references

The examples are short, corrected sentences of the kind a programmer writes
at work or to an AI assistant.

## Principles

- Simple, natural English that people really say at work or in chat.
- One idea per sentence, at most 20 words.
- Practise one point at a time: word order, an article, a tense, a preposition.
- Topics from the learner's life: programming, a project, git, working with AI.
- No rare words and no idioms that only native speakers know.

## Examples

- Tell me what the next step is. - word order in a question inside a sentence
- Just tell me; don't implement it. - two orders in one sentence, "it" as the object
- Let's continue with the next step. - "continue with", and "the" before "next"
- I have committed, and we are ready for the next step. - present perfect for a finished action
- Do you want me to press Resume before or after I commit? - "before/after" + a clause
- Everything looks good. - "everything" takes a singular verb
- I agree with your proposal for the button. - "agree with", "proposal for"
- Is the next step Review, or am I wrong? - a short question at the end
